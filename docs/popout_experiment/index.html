Pop-out search experiment

Diamonds will flash briefly on the screen, and you will report which side of one diamond is cut off by pressing Z (left) or M (right). It takes about 10–15 minutes.

  • Use a laptop or desktop computer with a physical keyboard.
  • Sit in a quiet place and close other tabs and programs.
  • The page will switch to full screen. Don't switch windows until you're finished.
  • At the end you'll download a data file. Keep it, because that file is your data.

This looks like a touch device. The experiment needs a physical keyboard.
